This Agreement is made on 28 August 2026 between Greater Bay Receivables Finance Co., Ltd. (the "Financier") and Lingnan Homeware Manufacturing Co., Ltd. (the "Client").

1. THE FACILITY

1.1 The Financier provides the Client with an invoice financing facility with a facility limit of RMB 350,000.

1.2 Advance rate: the Financier advances 80% of the face amount of each eligible invoice.

1.3 An invoice is eligible if it is issued to a buyer approved by the Financier, confirmed by that buyer and not overdue.

2. DISBURSEMENT

2.1 Each advance is disbursed within three (3) days of an accepted financing request.

3. CHARGES

3.1 Interest: the interest rate is 7.8% per annum on the amount advanced.

3.2 Service fee: a service fee of 0.25% of each advance is charged for each period of thirty (30) days, or part of such a period, for which the advance is outstanding.

4. REPAYMENT

4.1 Each advance is repaid from the buyer's payment of the financed invoice, which the Client shall direct to the collection account of the Financier.

5. SECURITY

5.1 No personal guarantee, collateral or pledge is required under this Agreement.

6. GOVERNING LAW

6.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China.
