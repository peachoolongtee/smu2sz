This Agreement is made on 25 August 2026 between Pearl River Commercial Bank, Foshan Branch (the "Bank") and Lingnan Homeware Manufacturing Co., Ltd. (the "Borrower").

1. THE FACILITY

1.1 The Bank makes available to the Borrower a revolving working-capital credit line with a facility limit of RMB 150,000.

1.2 The Borrower shall use each drawing for its general working capital.

2. DRAWDOWN

2.1 Drawdowns are funded on the same day as the drawdown request.

2.2 The Borrower may repay and redraw any part of the credit line during its availability.

3. INTEREST AND FEES

3.1 The interest rate is 6.4% per annum, accruing daily on the amount drawn.

3.2 No arrangement fee, commitment fee or other fee is payable for the credit line.

4. REPAYMENT

4.1 Each drawing is repaid in a single bullet payment at the end of its interest period, and may be redrawn.

5. FINANCIAL COVENANTS

5.1 Debt service coverage: the Borrower shall maintain a debt service coverage ratio (DSCR) of at least 1.25 times in each month.

5.2 Leverage: the total debt of the Borrower shall not exceed 2.5 times its annual EBITDA.

5.3 Clean-down: the outstanding balance of the credit line shall be reduced to zero for at least thirty (30) consecutive days in each year.

6. SECURITY

6.1 No personal guarantee, collateral or pledge is required under this Agreement.

7. GOVERNING LAW

7.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China.
