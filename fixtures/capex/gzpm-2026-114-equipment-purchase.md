This Equipment Purchase Contract (the "Contract") is made on 8 October 2026 between:

(1) Guangzhou Precision Machinery Co., Ltd., a company registered in Guangzhou, Guangdong (the "Seller"); and

(2) Lingnan Homeware Manufacturing Co., Ltd., a company registered in Foshan, Guangdong (the "Buyer").

The Seller and the Buyer are each a "Party" and together the "Parties".

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Equipment" means the automatic die-cutting line described in Schedule 1, with its tooling and control software.

1.2 "Delivery" means the arrival of the Equipment at the Buyer's factory in Shunde, Foshan.

1.3 "Acceptance" means the Buyer's written acceptance of the Equipment after the acceptance test in clause 5.

2. EQUIPMENT

2.1 The Seller shall deliver, install and commission the Equipment at the Buyer's factory in Shunde, Foshan.

2.2 The Equipment shall be delivered within sixty (60) days after the date of this Contract.

3. PRICE

3.1 The Contract Price is RMB 600,000 (six hundred thousand yuan).

3.2 The Contract Price includes delivery, installation, commissioning, operator training and value-added tax.

4. PAYMENT

4.1 30% of the Contract Price is payable on signing of this Contract.

4.2 60% of the Contract Price is payable within forty-five (45) days after delivery of the Equipment.

4.3 The Buyer shall retain 10% of the Contract Price as retention for twelve (12) months after Acceptance, as security for the Seller's warranty obligations.

4.4 The Seller shall invoice each payment, and the Buyer shall pay by bank transfer to the account of the Seller stated on the invoice.

4.5 If the Buyer pays the delivery payment early, within five (5) days of Delivery, the Seller grants a discount of 2% of that payment.

4.6 Overdue amounts bear interest at 0.05% per day.

5. ACCEPTANCE

5.1 The Seller shall test the Equipment at the Buyer's factory within thirty (30) days after Delivery, in the presence of the Buyer.

5.2 The Equipment shall conform to the specifications in Schedule 1.

6. WARRANTY

6.1 The Seller warrants the Equipment for twelve (12) months after Acceptance and shall repair or replace any defective part at its own cost.

7. AMENDMENT

7.1 No amendment to this Contract is valid unless it is made in writing and signed by both Parties.

8. GOVERNING LAW

8.1 This Contract is governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China.
