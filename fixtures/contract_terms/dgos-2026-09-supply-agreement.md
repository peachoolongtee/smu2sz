This Supply Agreement (the "Agreement") is made on 28 September 2026 between:

(1) Lingnan Homeware Manufacturing Co., Ltd., a company registered in Foshan, Guangdong (the "Supplier"); and

(2) Dongguan Office Supplies Ltd., a company registered in Dongguan, Guangdong (the "Buyer").

The Supplier and the Buyer are each a "Party" and together the "Parties".

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Batch" means one delivery of Products under this Agreement.

1.2 "Products" means the office furniture described in Schedule 2.

1.3 "Invoice Date" means the date printed on the Supplier's invoice for a Batch.

2. TERM

2.1 This Agreement takes effect on 1 October 2026 and continues for a term of twenty-four (24) months, until 30 September 2028.

3. PRICE

3.1 The Buyer shall pay the Supplier RMB 180,000 (one hundred and eighty thousand yuan) for each Batch.

4. INVOICING AND PAYMENT

4.1 The Supplier shall invoice each Batch on the day it is delivered. Batches are delivered every thirty (30) days, the first regular Batch on 15 October 2026.

4.2 Each invoice is payable forty-five (45) days after the Invoice Date.

4.3 The Buyer shall pay by bank transfer to the account of the Supplier stated on the invoice.

4.4 Early settlement. The Buyer may settle any invoice early, within three (3) days of the Supplier's written request, at a discount of 1.0% of the invoice amount.

5. DELIVERY

5.1 The Supplier shall deliver each Batch of Products to the Buyer's warehouse in Songshan Lake, Dongguan.

6. AMENDMENT

6.1 No amendment to this Agreement is valid unless it is made in writing and signed by both Parties.

7. GOVERNING LAW

7.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China.
