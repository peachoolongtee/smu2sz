# public_sme: the source trail

The books in `public_sme.json` (state and mandate) and `public_sme.mandate.json` (the unsigned mandate the pilot
runtime signs) are built from **one public filing** of a small Guangdong manufacturer, anonymised. Every figure below is
either **from the filing** (page and table named), **derived** from the filing by a stated formula, **composite** (a
second public filing, named), or **calibrated** (a survey or the golden books, named). No company, bank or person
name from the filing appears in the fixture: buyers are `Buyer A`…`Buyer E` and `Other buyers (aggregate)`, lenders
are `Main bank`, `Guaranteed-loan bank`, `Supply-chain finance platform`. Money is integer fen, rates are basis points.
The fixture is regenerated by the scratch build script described in §9; the arithmetic is reproduced here.

## 3. Rates: derived from the filing's accrued interest, checked against two peers' stated rates

The filing does not print the contract rates. It prints the accrued, unpaid interest at 31 December on each loan
(未到期应付利息): ¥11,916.67 on the ¥15,000,000 long-term loan and ¥17,600.00 on the ¥21,000,000 of short-term loans.
Chinese bank loans settle interest monthly on the 20th/21st on a 360-day basis, so ten days had accrued:

- long-term loan: 15,000,000 × r × 10/360 = 11,916.67 → **r = 2.86 %** exactly (15,000,000 × 0.0286 = 429,000; /36 = 11,916.67). Books: `D-LT-MORT` `rate_bp` 286.
- short-term loans: 21,000,000 × r × 10/360 = 17,600 → r ≈ 3.02 % blended; read as **3.00 %** on the ¥15m credit loans (12,500.00) and **3.06 %** on the ¥6m mortgage-backed loan (5,100.00), which sum to 17,600.00. Books: `D-ST-CREDIT` 300 bp, `D-ST-MORT` 306 bp, `FAC-LINE` 300 bp (the undrawn room of the same lines).

Cross-check: interest expense for the year ¥1,104,598.52 (income statement p. 35) on average borrowings of about
¥36.6m is 3.0 %. Two other Guangdong NEEQ manufacturers' 2025 annual reports print their contract rates for the same
months: 深圳行健 (430467, Shenzhen, report of 22 April 2026, note 短期借款 pp. 99–100) 2.40 %–3.00 % on loans from six
Shenzhen bank branches; 电通微电 (830976, Shenzhen, report of 29 April 2026, note 短期借款) 2.35 %, 3.10 %, 3.70 % and
4.25 %, the last two guaranteed by the municipal SME guarantee company. The derived 2.86–3.06 % sits inside that range.

Interest is scheduled on day 20 (21 January) and every 30 days (`next_payment_day` 20, `payment_every_days` 30), as
interest only: `monthly_debt_service_fen` = principal × rate × 30/365, rounded to the fen (the engine's `line_interest`):
D-ST-CREDIT 3,698,630; D-ST-MORT 1,509,041; D-LT-MORT 3,526,027. All three maturities are placed **beyond the 120-day
horizon**: the filing gives no maturity dates, Chinese short-term loans are 12-month, and the cash-flow statement shows
every loan repaid and re-lent within the year (取得借款 44,200,000, 偿还债务 45,400,000, p. 39). **Calibrated.** A first
build placed the ¥6m mortgage-backed loan's maturity on day 100 with no rollover; that second breach (days 104–113) kept
the day-12 line draw unaffordable to repay until the horizon, and the cash price curve then refused (its ceiling is two
30-day periods of the marginal source, §9): a finding for the pilot, not a reason to keep an invented date.

## 9. Verification on day 0 (the first dry-pilot reading)

Scratch script (`load_scenario("public_sme")`, `project` in CONSERVATIVE mode over 120 days, `alerts`), then the pilot
runtime script on port 8040 with `FEATURE_ENVELOPE=1 FEATURE_PRICE_CURVE=1 FEATURE_COLLECTIONS_WORTH=1
FEATURE_PILOT_JOURNAL=1`, `DEMO_MODE=0`, the state alone as `--state` and `public_sme.mandate.json` as `--mandate`
(signed with the published demo key: a local, read-only reading). `/state` answered `org SME-PUBLIC-1`, day 0, cash
¥7,634,503.82; `/mandate` signature VALID, validity ACTIVE; `/health` showed the four flags on.

**Projection.** Lowest closing cash −¥637,686.75 on day 24; first breach of the floor on day 14 (payroll and VAT on
the 15th), 11 breach days (14–24); the ¥10.5m aggregate receipt on day 25 restores it; cash builds to about ¥22m by day
50 as the opening receivables convert at their contractual due days, and ends the horizon near ¥18m. Leverage 3.68×
every month, DSCR 9.3×. Marginal cost of funds 3.00 % (FAC-LINE); break-even early-payment discount at 30 days 0.24 %.

**Alerts (4).**

- CRITICAL `RESERVE_BREACH_AHEAD`: "Reserve breach ahead on day 14. Projected cash closes at ¥2,047,710.19 on day 14,
  ¥1,596,719.80 below the reserve floor; the gap peaks at ¥4,282,116.74 on day 24 and lasts to day 24. A committed cure
  exists: DRAW_FACILITY FAC-LINE ¥4,282,116.74."
- WARNING `RECEIVABLE_PAST_DUE` ×3: "Receivable RCV-B1 is 35 day(s) past due. ¥3,000,000.00 outstanding from BUYER-B was
  due on day -35 and is still OPEN. The conservative trajectory no longer counts on a dated receipt." Likewise RCV-F1,
  20 days, ¥7,500,000.00 (Other buyers) and RCV-D1, 10 days, ¥2,549,267.35 (Buyer D).

**The three routes (day 0, English; all 200).**

- `GET /buyers/BUYER-A/envelope` (what can be offered to the largest customer): "You can live with: early payment at up
  to 0.10% on ¥5,397,202.57; a rebate up to 1.50%; no longer payment terms." Reason: "No payment-terms step fails
  inside the range FLOW checks." Money: "Without a deal your current funding plan costs ¥8,094.96." (The sweep's range
  ends at 90 days, which is Buyer A's current term, so "no longer payment terms" is the range's edge, not a wall; the
  concession budget of ¥100,000 is not what binds.)
- `GET /cash/price-curve`: "Cash in the next 24 days saves you ¥18.90 of funding cost per ¥10,000 (0.19%); after that,
  earlier cash saves nothing." Bands: days 0–24 ¥18.90 (19 bp), days 25–120 ¥0.
- `GET /receivables/worth` (who to chase): "Call Other buyers (aggregate) today for the ¥7,500,000.00: it saves you
  ¥8,094.96 and is the only free action." Ranking: RCV-F1 ¥8,094.96, RCV-B1 ¥5,671.23, RCV-D1 ¥4,819.16, RCV-F2
  ¥1,659.67, RCV-A1 ¥1,659.67, RCV-C1 ¥1,311.81, RCV-B2 ¥930.67, RCV-E1 ¥893.74; first breach day 14.

A reading of the reading: the books say what the filing says in words, that the company lives on its credit lines
(¥44m borrowed and ¥45m repaid in the year) while a third of its receivables run late. The agent's first answers are to
chase the three late invoices (any one of the three large ones clears the breach, so the plan's whole ¥8,094.96 cost is
the saving) and otherwise to bridge the 15th with a ¥4.28m draw on the line at 3 %; terms to the largest customer are
already at the edge of what is scanned, and an early-payment discount is worth at most 0.10 % because the line is cheap.
Against the golden books (marginal 10.84 %, break-even 0.88 %) every money figure is a quarter the size: the pilot
should expect Chinese bank-financed SMEs to read like this.
