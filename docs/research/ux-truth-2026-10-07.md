## 1. Invoice-financing timing (brief 2A)

Recorded (`apps/web/mocks/verdict_ex01.json` → `plan`, identical to `cure_plan` and to `violations[RESERVE_BREACH].inputs.cure_plan`):

- `label` "committed plan: ¥2,406.67 (cannot run today)", `executable_now: false`, `not_executable_reason` "… RI:BUYER-1:32 is not an invoice the executor can finance today; it is issued on day 32".
- `actions[0]`: `FINANCE_INVOICE`, `FAC-IF`, `day: 0`, `amount_fen: 13504000`, `receivable_id: "RI:BUYER-1:32"`, `lands_day: 3`.
- `costs[0].formula`: "135,040.00 × 7.8% × 60/365 (57 days billed as 2 × 30-day period) = 1,731.47; 135,040.00 × 0.25% × 2 = 675.20; total ¥2,406.67".

Engine, re-run on the golden scenario (`project(state, bound_obligations, cure_plan.actions, CONSERVATIVE, …)`): the only ADVANCE posting is on **day 35**, `FAC-IF`, 13,504,000 fen, formula "requested day 32 + 3-day lead time". The invoice is due on day 92 under the offer's 60-day terms, so 92 − 35 = 57 days, which is exactly the "57 days billed as 2 × 30-day period" in the cost line. The cost is therefore priced on the engine's landing day. `test_golden_verdicts.py::test_ex01_reserve_breach_day_47_curable` (l. 61–90) states the same in its comment ("submitted on its issue day, lands day 35, due day 92").

Why `lands_day` says 3: `router.py::_with_landing` (l. 254–266) does `next((r for r in search.state.receivables if r.id == a.receivable_id), None)`; the state has one receivable, `RCV-A` (`fixtures/scenarios/golden_sme.json`), while `RI:BUYER-1:32` is synthesised from `recurring_invoices` (`next_issue_day: 2`, `every_days: 30`) by `search.py::receivables` (l. 117–134) into `search.rcvs`. With `r is None`, `issue = a.day = 0`, and `invoice_landing_day(FAC-IF, 0, 0) = max(0, 0) + 3 = 3`. The pricing path (`search.py::action_pricing`, l. 535) uses `self.rcvs` and gets 35. `FundingAction.lands_day` is documented as "Display only: no figure depends on it, and the engine ignores it" (`domain/models.py` l. 524–526); `tests/api/test_api_gaps.py::test_lands_day_agrees_with_the_engine` covers the standing plan on current books (RCV-A, issued day −13, lands day 3) and never an advance on an unissued recurring invoice, which is why the gap is untested.

What `day: 0` means: it is the request day the router priced the plan on (the books' `as_of_day`), not a day anything happens. The executor would refuse the leg today (`search.py::unrunnable`, l. 543–556: the invoice is not in `financeable`), and the engine's own rule is "an unissued invoice is submitted on its issue day". Business mode should say: "This can be fixed for ¥2,406.67 by financing the invoice that is issued in 32 days; the cash arrives in 35 days. Nothing to run today." The Deals page today reads "Cash drops below your minimum cash reserve: fixable for ¥2,406.67." (`glance.cure`) with no mention of when.

## 2. Funding ranking (brief 2B)

The smallest addition is `shortfall_fen: int | None` on `ComparisonRow`, filled from `ev.shortfall` (and `displayed()` in `app/api/display.py` l. 37–38 produces `shortfall_display` automatically).

One wrinkle for a truthful UI: `executable_now` is `True` on `line_guaranteed_loan` because `_row` sets `executable_now = ev is None or not ev.unrunnable` (l. 306) and the row has no evaluation. Read it only on admissible rows.

## Fields needed

The smallest set of presentation fields a truthful UI needs, each a copy of a number the engine already produces:

1. `FundingAction.lands_day` correct for an advance on an unissued recurring invoice (`_with_landing` reading `search.rcvs`); it is 35 for the EX01 cure, not 3.
2. `ComparisonRow.shortfall_fen` (from `Eval.shortfall`) and, optionally, `worst_day`; `displayed()` yields `shortfall_display`.
3. `/home` `cash.lowest_fen / lowest_day / lowest_when` and `worst` computed over `d.day >= today`.
4. Per-counter `components: [{obligation_id, type, value_fen}]` and `net_fen` in the verdict view (from `value_concessions` on the counter's obligations); `supplier_cost_fen` unchanged.
5. `TrajectoryView.first_breach_cash` (or a documented read of `violations[RESERVE_BREACH].observed`).
6. In the web app: `recommendationKey(outcome, countersCount)`; the deal list row shows the recommendation words; `OutcomeBadge` only inside disclosures and Demo views.
