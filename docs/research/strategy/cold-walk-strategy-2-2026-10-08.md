## 1. The three questions

**"What can I offer this buyer?"** — Yes, one reading. Deals, Option A:
> "Terms stay at 45 days, no rebate. We will accept early settlement on up to ¥257,200 of this invoice at 0.85%, paid within 2 days."
In my words: say no to longer terms and the rebate; let them pay about ¥257k of the invoice now with a 0.85% discount. Cost to me ¥3,027.77, vs ¥3,113.93 if I just borrow.
Hesitated on: why ¥257,200? It is an odd number that appears everywhere and is never explained on the card (I only worked out later it is the amount the funding would otherwise cover). "Open Option B" is a button with no sentence — I have no idea what B is without clicking.

**"What is cash arriving earlier worth to me?"** — Yes. Cash day 0:
> "Cash in the next 30 days saves you ¥89.11 of funding cost per ¥10,000 (0.89%); after that, earlier cash saves nothing."
In my words: every ¥10k that lands inside the next month saves me about ¥89 of borrowing; after the month, no gain. Day 34 version ("¥178.22 ... (1.78%), then ¥89.12 (0.89%) for the 18 days after; later, nothing") reads the same way — more urgent in the first 8 days.
Hesitated on: "per ¥10,000" makes me do arithmetic to apply it to a ¥162,800 invoice; the Receivables "Saves ¥…" cell does that for me, so the two together work. Tiny itch: ¥89.11 on day 0 vs ¥89.12 on day 34.

**"Whom should I chase first, and how?"** — Yes. Receivables day 34, top row, and repeated on Home:
> "Call Foshan Home Retail Co. today for the ¥162,800.00: it saves you ¥1,374.61 and is the only free action."
In my words: phone them today about the late ¥162.8k; it is worth ~¥1.4k and costs nothing. Next row says offer early payment on the ¥420k due in 13 days; third row says wait.
Hesitated on: all three invoices are the same buyer, so "whom" is trivial in the demo; the ranking still reads correctly. Row 3 says "Saves nothing extra now" and then in small print "If it arrived today it would save you ¥1,706.10" — those two sentences fought each other for a second (see §5).

## 2. First impression of the Cash sentence
A benefit to me, clearly. "saves you ¥89.11 of funding cost per ¥10,000 (0.89%)" — the verb "saves you" and "funding cost" anchor it; the percentage in brackets now reads as "that is 0.89% of the money", not a fee. Good fix. Day 34: "saves you ¥178.22 ... (1.78%)" — same, benefit.
