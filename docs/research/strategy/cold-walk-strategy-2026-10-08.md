# Cold walk: strategy MVP screenshots (8 Oct 2026)

Who I am: I run a small trading business. I have never seen FLOW. I looked only at the
nine desktop shots and the two phone shots named in the brief. I did not open any code.

## 1. The three questions

### "What can I offer this buyer?" (Deals, card "Before they ask")
Sentence I relied on: "Up to 55-day terms if the price rises 0.30%, or early payment at up
to 0.85% on ¥257,200.00; a rebate up to 1.25%; not 60 days without an early payment."

What I took it to mean: I can stretch Foshan to 55 days if I put the price up 0.30%; or I can
let them pay about ¥257k of the invoice early for a 0.85% discount; I can give a rebate of up
to 1.25%; and I must not give 60 days unless they pay early. Can I answer in one reading? Half.
I got the four pieces, but I had to re-read to see that the "or" separates two alternatives
and the semicolons separate independent levers. I could not tell whether the three levers are
meant to be combined or are either/or.

Where I hesitated: "¥257,200.00" appears from nowhere; the invoice is ¥420,000. Nothing on the
card says why only ¥257k. I also misread "not 60 days without an early payment" the first time
as "no, 60 days, without early payment" (a sentence fragment), and only on the second pass saw
it is a refusal. The second line, "60 days leaves a shortfall in 47 days that no cure covers;
the ¥5,000.00 budget buys no terms without a price step", I did not understand at all on the
first reading; "cure" and "price step" stopped me.

### "What is cash arriving earlier worth to me?" (Cash & funding, line under headroom)
Day 0: "Cash arriving within the next 30 days is worth 0.89% to you; after that, nothing."
Day 34: "Cash arriving within the next 8 days is worth 1.78% to you, then 0.89% if it arrives
within the next 26 days; after that, nothing."

What I took it to mean (after thinking): if a customer pays me within the window, each yuan is
worth about an extra 0.9 fen to me because I will not have to borrow it. Can I answer in one
reading? No. My honest first reading was that 0.89% is something I pay (see question 4), and
"after that, nothing" read as "money arriving after 30 days is worth nothing", which is
obviously false and made me distrust the line. There is no yuan figure next to it; I had to go
to Receivables to find any money amount.

### "Whom should I chase first, and how?" (Receivables, day 34)
Sentence I relied on: "Call Foshan Home Retail Co. today for the ¥162,800.00: it saves you
¥1,374.61 and is the only free action." plus the column "Worth collecting" and the chips
"Call now / Offer early payment / Wait".

What I took it to mean: ring Foshan about the late ¥162,800 first; it costs me nothing and is
worth about ¥1,375. Then, if I want, offer early payment on the ¥420k due in 13 days. Leave
the other ¥420k alone. Can I answer in one reading? Yes for the first row, which is clear and
is the best sentence in the whole product. No for the second row: its Action text is a
48-word sentence with five percentages and three yuan amounts and I gave up on it.

   - Deals Details table: "Early payment on ¥420,000.00 ... 0.70% on ¥420,000.00" and "Early

   Four ceilings (0.70 / 0.75 / 0.85 / 0.88) and three amounts (¥405k / ¥257.2k / ¥420k) for

## 3. Words I did not understand (and what I would call them)
- "Hypothetical" (badge on the Deals card): sounds like "made up". Say "If they ask" or
  "Not sent, for planning".
- "cure" / "no cure covers": no idea. Say "no funding covers it" or "nothing we have plugs it".
- "price step": say "price increase".
- "break-even 0.88%": say "the discount that costs the same as borrowing".
- "standing plan": say "the funding plan you already have" or "Plan #1 on Cash & funding".
- "Terms and price zone": say "Terms vs price table".
- "approved" / "not approved" / "needs your approval" / "its funding cannot run today": four
  statuses that overlap. Say "within your limits" / "over your limits" / "cannot be funded yet".
- "RCV-A", "RI:BUYER-1:2", "RI:BUYER-1:32": codes under buyer names. Use my invoice numbers.
- "headroom", "first breach": say "spare cash", "first day below your reserve".
- "Lever" (column heading in Details): say "What we could give".
- "Or finance it." (tail of the Receivables sentence): finance what? Say "Or borrow against it".
- "lowest point" and "worst point" both appear; pick one.

## 4. First impression of "0.89%" / "1.78%"
A rate I am charged. "Cash arriving ... is worth 0.89% to you" looked like an interest or
financing rate, the kind of number a bank quotes. Only when I reached Receivables and saw
"saves you ¥1,374.61" did I work out it is a benefit. The word "worth" is not strong enough
against a bare percentage; a yuan figure ("worth about ¥3,700 if the ¥420k arrives early")
would have landed first time. "after that, nothing" also reads as a threat, not a fact.

## 5. Does the Deals card read as my limits or as an offer?
It reads as an offer, and as an offer of the maximum. "Up to 55-day terms", "up to 0.85%",
"a rebate up to 1.25%" are phrased like a menu I am about to hand over. The buttons under it
say "Open Option A / Open Option B", and Option A below is exactly the card's ceiling ("early
settlement on up to ¥257,200 of this invoice at 0.85%"). So the top of my range becomes the
first thing I offer. Nothing on the card says "this is the most you could give; start lower".
The buyer asked for 1.2%, 60 days and a 1% rebate; the card volunteers 0.85%, 55 days and a
1.25% rebate, which is more rebate than they asked for. Yes, it feels like it is nudging me to
give more on the rebate. The card title "Before they ask" plus "Hypothetical" does not say
whether this is private advice or a message draft.

## 6. Receivables action order
Would I follow it? Row 1 yes: call the late customer, free, ¥1,374.61. Row 2 maybe: I would
not offer early payment at 0.80% on ¥200,000 to a customer who is already two days late on
another invoice without the page telling me to raise that in the same call. Row 3 "Wait" yes,
but see below. "Call now / Offer early payment / Wait" as chips are clear, and the colours
(red row for late) help. The ranking caption "Ranked by what collecting each saves you, net of
any discount that earns it" is understandable but long; "Ranked by what collecting saves you"
would do.

"¥77.58" next to "¥1,706.10 before any discount": it does not make sense. It reads as "this
invoice is worth ¥1,706 to collect but the discount leaves me ¥77", which says the discount
eats 95% of the benefit; then why is the action "Offer early payment"? Both ¥420k rows show the
identical "¥1,706.10 before any discount", which is also the whole cost of plan #1, so it looks
like a copied constant, not something about that invoice. And the 43-day row shows ¥73.68
"worth collecting" while the Action says "saves nothing extra". Pick one.

## 8. Five most important findings, ranked
1. (Deals / Home / Receivables) One invoice, four early-payment ceilings and three amounts
   (0.70/0.75/0.85/0.88%; ¥420k/¥405k/¥257.2k). Fix: one number per day, shown identically on
   every page, with one line saying why the amount is less than the invoice.
2. (Cash & funding) "worth 0.89% to you" reads as a charge and does not match any yuan figure
   on Receivables; "after that, nothing" reads as false. Fix: "If the ¥420k arrives within 30
   days it saves you about ¥3,700 in funding cost; later than that it saves nothing." Put the
   same yuan figure in the Receivables row.
3. (Deals card) The card hands me my maximums as the offer, including a rebate above what the
   buyer asked for. Fix: retitle "The most we can give" and add a line "Open with less: e.g.
   50 days at +0.15%"; keep the ceilings in Details.
4. (Receivables) "¥77.58 / ¥1,706.10 before any discount" and the "Wait" row's ¥73.68 next to
   "saves nothing extra". Fix: show one figure per row, "Saves you ¥X", and show ¥0 with
   "Wait" when the action saves nothing. Drop the repeated "¥1,706.10 before any discount".
5. (Deals Details) "Approved packages" that "need your approval" and "cannot run today"; plus
   "cure", "price step", "Hypothetical". Fix: three plain statuses ("within your limits" /
   "needs your sign-off" / "no funding for it yet"), replace "cure" with "funding", "price
   step" with "price increase", and change the badge to "Not sent".
