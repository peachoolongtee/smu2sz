DOMESTIC TRACKS
Domestic Competition Rules
All team members must be full-time students, including undergraduate, master’s and doctoral students, with no restrictions on major or year of study. The Domestic Track is open to students enrolled at universities in mainland China, Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan, as well as international students, exchange students and visiting students enrolled at universities in China. Students enrolled at overseas universities, including Chinese nationals, should enter the International Track.
Team formation
Only team entries are accepted. Each team must have 2–5 members, and information for every member must be completed before submission. Cross-year, cross-major and cross-university teams are allowed.
Information authenticity
Participants must ensure that all registration information is truthful, accurate and valid. Each person may join only one team; duplicate registration is invalid.
Independent completion and integrity
Teams must complete their entries independently and comply with applicable laws and regulations. Cheating, fraud and infringement of others’ intellectual property rights are prohibited. If a violation is discovered or reported and verified during the competition, the Organising Committee may revoke the team’s eligibility and results.
Intellectual property
The intellectual property rights of competition entries belong to the participants. The organiser may use entries, related entry information and team information in promotional materials, publications, designated and authorised media, official website display and download, and exhibitions including touring exhibitions.
Fees and finalist support
No registration fee is charged. Accommodation and round-trip transportation for invited finalists during the final will be arranged by the Organising Committee in accordance with policies announced in subsequent notices.

The competition is open to full-time undergraduate, master’s and doctoral students enrolled at overseas universities or research institutes, regardless of nationality, major or year of study. “Overseas universities” means higher education institutions and research institutes established outside China (including Hong Kong, Macao and Taiwan), with independent educational qualifications and the ability to confer academic qualifications or degrees.
Team formation
Participation is team-based. Each team must have 2–5 members. Cross-year, cross-major, cross-university and cross-country teams are allowed. Non-Chinese nationals must account for at least 60% of each team. The Organising Committee will determine track eligibility based on members’ nationalities, institutions and supporting documents. A team that does not meet the International Track requirements may be reassigned to the appropriate track or disqualified.
Important note: To reflect the international character of the International Track, teams are encouraged to demonstrate diversity and international representation in their membership and to include students of different nationalities and cultural backgrounds. Where the calculation does not result in a whole number, the minimum is rounded up to the next whole person (for example, a 3-member team must include at least 2 non-Chinese members).
Registration and information authenticity
During registration, teams must truthfully provide information for every member, including name, country of nationality, type and number of a valid personal identification document such as a passport or other valid identity document, institution and proof of enrolment or student status, major, year of study and contact details. All information must be truthful, accurate and valid. Each person may join only one team across the Domestic and International Tracks; duplicate registration is invalid.
Official language
English is the official language of the International Track. Entry submission, presentations and judging are conducted in English, and submitted materials must include an English version.
Independent completion and integrity
Teams must complete their entries independently and comply with applicable laws and regulations. Cheating, fraud, plagiarism and infringement of others’ intellectual property rights are prohibited. Judging Criteria
Each domestic track follows criteria appropriate to its competition format.
All six International Track topics use the same core judging dimensions.

DOMESTIC TRACK
Artificial Intelligence Track
Task Completion
Completion of scripted financial tasks, quality of planning and correctness of tool use.
Defence against induced transfers, information extraction, privilege escalation and prompt injection; confirmation and risk warnings for sensitive operations.
Novelty, real-world value, dialogue efficiency, clarification, error recovery and handling of colloquial input.
DOMESTIC TRACK
Data Analytics Track
Data Understanding & Modelling Analysis
Assesses the completeness of data introduction and the quality of data cleaning; the depth of business-pain-point discovery and visual communication in descriptive analysis; and the rationale of feature engineering and model selection, rigour of algorithmic logic, and standardisation of the modelling process.
Application Validation & Effect Evaluation
Assesses the scientific soundness of the validation plan, such as offline evaluation, back-testing and cross-validation; whether evaluation metrics fit the business context; model interpretability; and robustness in real-world environments.
Product Thinking & Innovation Value
Assesses the feasibility of scenario implementation, the logical closed loop of the product solution and interaction design; the originality of the problem-solving perspective, methodological transfer or integration, and potential value and scalability in real financial or business scenarios.
考察典型金融场景任务的完成情况、多步骤任务规划合理性及工具调用正确性。
考察对诱导转账、信息套取、越权操作和 Prompt 注入等风险的防御能力，以及敏感操作确认和风险提示。
考察场景新颖性、真实问题解决度、对话轮次效率、追问能力、错误恢复、口语化输入和超范围兜底。
考察数据介绍的完整性与清洗规范性；考察描述性分析对业务痛点的挖掘深度与可视化表达；考察特征工程与模型选择的合理性、算法逻辑的严谨性及建模流程的标准化程度。
考察验证方案的科学性（如离线评估、回溯测试、交叉验证等），评估指标选择是否贴合业务场景，模型结果的可解释性及在真实环境下的鲁棒性表现。
考察场景落地的可行性、产品方案的逻辑闭环与交互体验设计；考察问题解决视角的新颖性、方法论迁移或融合的创新度，以及对金融/业务真实场景的潜在提升价值与推广意义。
Task Completion
Completion of scripted tasks in a simulated environment for non-on-chain topics; Topic F is evaluated using on-chain transaction hashes.
Defence against induced transfers, data extraction, privilege escalation and prompt injection; serious security failures trigger substantial deductions.
Scenario novelty, real-world value, dialogue efficiency, clarification, correction and fallback capability.
非链上方向以模拟环境中的脚本化任务完成度为准；方向 F 以真实链上交易哈希为依据。
统一考察对诱导转账、信息套取、越权操作和 Prompt 注入的防御能力；严重安全问题将触发大幅扣分。
考察场景新颖性、真实问题解决度、轮次效率、追问、纠错和兜底能力。
