## Owner's rules (decided 12 Oct, #89)
- A shortfall on a financed invoice is owed by the owner (recourse; the engine's `orphaned` rule).
- A late receipt on a financed invoice keeps the financing running; charges accrue per period exactly as the facility record says (`fee_bp_per_advance` / `fee_period_days`, `rate_bp`): never assume the repayment moves with the receipt without the charges.
- An early-payment agreement on an invoice paid late lapses (as today).
- "Due soon" = within 7 days before due; "Chase payment" leads from the day after due, no grace; a missed promise counts as overdue.
- Financing has three states, never mixed: ARRANGED (the lot exists, not landed), RECEIVED (landed: cash in the account from that day), REPAID (the receipt cleared the claim). Never the word "advanced" for an unlanded lot.
