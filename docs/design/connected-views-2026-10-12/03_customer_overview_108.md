# #108 Customer overview: the read (server) and the page (web)
Read ../map-econ/common-web.md first. Board: CustomerOverview (v17).
## Server (branch `feat/customer-overview-api`, from dev after #107 merges): `GET /customers/{id}/overview?lang=&decision=&path=&invoice=`
Composed from existing reads, nothing recomputed: customer inputs (`inputs_from_store`), the receivables rows with #107's `net`, `financing_state`, `governed_by`, `early_payment`, `pending_counter`; the funding lots on the buyer's invoices; the open offers for the buyer (`open_offer` headline_short, the recommended path and its cost display, the authority sentence); the exposure read's `concentration`, `payments`, `stress`, `capacity` (FEATURE_EXPOSURE; null with a `note` when off); the activity entries for the buyer. Shape: `name`, `summary_text`; `figures[4]` {key, label, value_display, note, tone} (relationship value "about ¥1.09m" + "a year, estimated contribution"; outstanding; financing linked with its state word; share of what you are owed "one open invoice; not its share of your sales"); `decision` {eyebrow, text, primary{label, href}, secondary[]} or null with `none_text`; `economics` rows (annual sales + provenance word; gross margin + "assumed for the Demo, not confirmed by you" when the provenance is DEMO; contribution "under today's terms"; the open request as a share "the cost of keeping the same sales on softer terms, not profit gained"; classed as), `economics_note` (the "does not by itself justify" sentence), `edit_href`; `exposure` rows (payment behaviour; financing with state and expected day; paid 30 days late; the financing then; spare capacity) + `same_figures_note`; `history[]` {when_text, text, href?}; `context.back {label, href}` built from the query. Every string via t(lang); no ids; the share of sales never stated unless `customer_inputs` has it. Cache like `cached_picture` on (state_version, day, buyer, lang). Tests: golden en/zh; flag off; a buyer with no open deal (`decision` null); a buyer with no financing (NONE); the back link from the query.
## Web (branch `feat/customer-overview-web`): `/customers/[id]`
Not a tab. Header eyebrow + back link (`context.back`); h1 the name; the summary sentence. Four figure cards. The ONE COMMERCIAL DECISION card (amber rule) with the three actions: Review the counter (primary, to /deals with `decision`, `path`), View the invoices (/receivables with `customer`, `invoice`), Stress-test payment (/cash deep link); or the `none_text` line and the two remaining actions. Two panels: CUSTOMER ECONOMICS (rows + note + "Edit these facts" to the inputs sheet) and FINANCIAL EXPOSURE (rows + the same-figures note). RELATIONSHIP HISTORY rows. Every customer name in Deals, Receivables, Cash and Activity becomes a link here carrying the context (one `customerHref(id, ctx)` helper in lib). Tests: `e2e/customer-overview.spec.ts` (mock): the four figures; the decision card's primary; the back link restores `/deals?decision=…&path=…` with the path selected; the links from each page carry the context; flag off shows the economics panel's `note`; zh; phone; axe.
