## Section 1: the position

## Section 2: the attention card (the one raised surface)
The raised, frosted card is `attention.items[primary_key]`: the eyebrow ("1 deal needs your response", amber), the title, the facts row, the primary button (filled, the page's only filled primary) and the secondary. Beneath a hairline, `funding_line.text` with the two costs labelled ("Invoice-financing cost ¥1,069.32; the whole plan, with the ¥150k credit-line draw already made, ¥3,113.93") and `authority_text`. When the queue has more than one item, the others follow as a short ordered list under the card ("Also waiting: …"), never as more raised cards. When the queue is empty the card shrinks to `funding_line` alone under a quiet "No urgent action required." line. The old "Nothing to decide today" / next-step card is removed when `attention` is present.

## Section 3: the picture with the event rail
Two-column from `lg` (the picture left, the rail 312 px right), stacked on a phone (the rail below). The picture keeps the plan line (blue) and the counterfactual (dashed grey, labelled by `cash.counterfactual_label` in the legend), the reserve, the server axis; it drops the event labels on the plot and the chips; it gains numbered marks at the rail's events (the lowest point amber, landings green, outflows grey; red only for a breach). Above it: the scenario switch (`Expected payments` | `Foshan 30 days late`, shown only when `cash.stress_available`), and the window switch (34 / 60 / 120 as today). No Play in Business mode. The rail: numbered rows from `events_rail` (amount, label, when; the lowest row with its amber note); click selects (aria-pressed), hover or focus previews: the matching mark fills, and the panel under the picture shows the event's `explain` with its title; the selected row stays selected on leave. The cursor: on hover over the plot the dotted cursor follows the pointer with the series' figures as the Deals picture does; on leave it returns to the selected event. The late scenario: the stress series drawn dashed red for the plan line, the panel replaced by `assumption_text` + the stress sentence (lowest, breach days, need, `plan_text`), and the window widened to `window_days_needed` with a one-line note; switching back restores. Reduced motion: no transitions; `aria-live="polite"` announces the panel title once per selection.
