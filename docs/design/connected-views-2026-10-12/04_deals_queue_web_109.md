Read ../map-econ/common-web.md first. Board: DealsQueue (v17). Branch `feat/deals-queue`.
Server reads you have: `GET /deals` / the inbox read (the open offers, their status), `open_offer` (headline_short, headline_note, read_text, the picture with paths, the moments, authority, cost basis), `GET /offers/{id}/exposure?lang=` (FEATURE_EXPOSURE: buyer, paths[], concentration, payments, stress, capacity{before,after,spare}, band, flag; one read for all paths), decision records. ## Layout (board order)
1. Header: "Deals" + one sentence; four count tiles as tabs (needs your response / awaiting the buyer / agreed, in force / customer setup), `aria-selected`, the list filtered by the tab (URL `?tab=`).
2. The list: customer (link to /customers/{id} with context), request, cost as asked, FLOW recommends, status word. The selected row `aria-current`; the "agreed, in force" row is the terms in force (contract ref, days, rebate), never a pending counter's figures.
3. The detail (replaces in place on selection, 150–200 ms): eyebrow with the customer link; h2 = `headline_short` ("Foshan: counter recommended."); the ask sentence; WHY COUNTER? as two short sentences with a red left rule (the limit excess as one figure, the cash breach when and for how long); FLOW RECOMMENDS · OPTION A in the one blue card: the option sentence, "Estimated one-off cost ¥X, if <buyer> accepts", the authority sentence ("Within your mandate." / the owner-review wording), the one filled primary "Review and send the counter" (it opens the existing response flow, which binds the exact option and figures and re-checks the books: show the server's recheck sentence beside the button); then, ALWAYS VISIBLE under the card, "Cash on each path": the #105 paths picture as built (the moments, hover, cursor, the three columns), with the exposure band between the columns and the paid-late toggle per docs/design/exposure-surface-2026-10-12.md (the chart is the evidence behind "Why counter?", never hidden); then a `<details>`-style Disclosure "Compare accepting, countering and doing nothing" holding ONLY the table (Do nothing | If you accept as asked | If <buyer> accepts Option A; rows: cost now one-off; cost over 12 months on the terms; financing cost caused over 12 months; lowest cash in the window; a one-off payment FLOW could still cover alone; your limit; the share of what you are owed), the buyer's message and the calculations.
4. The aside WHY FOSHAN MATTERS: three facts only (contribution a year "· estimated"; owes you now; share of what you are owed), "View the customer profile ›", the context-not-a-promise sentence, "What if Foshan pays 30 days late? ›" (deep link into Cash with context), the keeps-selection note.
Phone: tiles as a horizontal scroll of pills; the list collapses to two lines per row; the detail below the list in the order heading, ask, why, the Option A card with the button, the chart, the disclosure; the aside after the detail.
