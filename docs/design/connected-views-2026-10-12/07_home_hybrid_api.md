## H1. The position (section 1 of the mockup)
- `cash.status_word` (sentence case: "Covered, but tight") and `cash.status_tone` (ok | warn | bad), from the same rule as the existing pill (the pill fields stay).
- `cash.headline_conditional`: the heading when the forecast depends on pending landings or receipts: "Your cash is covered on the current plan." (zh twin); else the existing headline; `cash.headline_note`: "Cash stays at or above your ¥300k minimum for the next 120 days, assuming the financing arrives in 3 days and Foshan pays in 32 days as scheduled. It touches the minimum in 30 days."; `cash.assumptions`: a list of sentences, one per pending landing (facility, amount, when) and per expected receipt inside the horizon (buyer, amount, when), plus "the scheduled payroll, supplier and debt payments as entered".
- `cash.facts`: three `{key, label, value_short, value_display, note, tone}`: `today` (cash today; note "¥250k above your minimum", from `cash_fen − min_cash_fen`), `lowest` (the lowest point ahead with its when; note "¥0 above your minimum" or "¥X below your minimum", tone warn at 0, bad below), `arriving` (the next landing or receipt: amount, source, when).

## H2. The attention queue (section 2): one primary at a time
- `attention`: `{items: [...], primary_key}` ranked by the rule of the review's table: cash unsafe (a reserve breach with a runnable cure) → the funding recommendation; a deal awaiting the owner's reply → the deal; an owner approval pending → the approval; a major customer overdue → the collection; a funding settlement pending → the landing; everything healthy → none. Each item: `key`, `kind` (funding | deal | approval | collection | landing), `eyebrow` ("1 deal needs your response"), `title` ("Foshan asked for 60-day terms and a 1% rebate. FLOW recommends countering with Option A."), `facts` (two to three `{label, value, tone}`: the counter's cost ¥3,027.77 once; "as asked: over your ¥5,000 limit"; "Foshan is 100% of what you are owed" only when the exposure flag is on, else omitted), `primary` `{label: "Review the counter", href: "/deals?id=…"}`, `secondary` `{label: "Why this?", href}`. `primary_key` names the one item that is the page's primary; the rest are the queue in order. When the queue is empty: `attention.none_text` "No urgent action required."
- `funding_line`: the plan's state as one sentence with the cost split: `state` ("arranged" | "landed" | "repaid" | "none"), `text` ("Funding arranged · ¥255k of invoice financing expected in 3 days, repaid from Foshan's receipt in 32 days."), `cost_leg_display` (the invoice-financing leg: ¥1,069.32), `cost_leg_label`, `cost_total_display` (the whole plan: ¥3,113.93), `cost_total_label` ("the whole plan, with the ¥150k credit-line draw already made"), `authority_text` ("Within your signed limits; nothing for you to do." or the approval sentence). Figures from the router's plan `costs` lines, never summed here beyond what `total_cost_fen` already is.

## H3. The event rail (section 3)
- `cash.events_rail`: the key events in the picture window, at most six, ascending: the N largest moves on the plan's line (landings, payroll, supplier payments, receipts, repayments; dedupe same-day) plus the lowest point as an event; each `{key, day, when, kind (landing | payroll | supplier | receipt | repayment | debt | lowest), label ("invoice financing arrives"), amount_short ("+¥255k", signed), cash_after_short, cash_after_display, room_after_short ("¥165k above your minimum" or "¥255k below"), tone, explain}` where `explain` is the panel sentence ("Cash falls from ¥805k to ¥465k. Your minimum is ¥300k, so ¥165k of room remains. Without the financing, payroll would have taken cash to ¥210k, below your minimum."); the lowest event's `explain` names both lines. A same-day receipt-and-repayment (day 32: Foshan pays ¥420k, ¥257k repays the financing, ¥163k reaches cash) is ONE event with the three figures in `explain` and `net_short`.
- `cash.counterfactual_label`: "without FLOW's financing plan (a comparison, not a choice you still have)" when a plan has run; "if you did nothing" only when no action has executed.
- `cash.stress_available`: true when the exposure flag is on and the buyer's stress can be read; the picture's late scenario then reads the stress series from `GET /offers/{id}/exposure` or, for Home, a new `cash.stress` on the home read: `{delay_days: 30, buyer, series (as the picture's series), lowest_short, lowest_when, breach_days, need_short, plan_text, assumption_text, window_days_needed}` (`window_days_needed` so the page widens the window to show the moved receipt). Behind the flag; omitted when off.

## H4. The cards (section 4)
- `deals_card`: one row per deal: `{buyer, status_text ("Your response needed" | "Awaiting the buyer" | "Agreed" | "Needs your approval"), request_text ("60-day terms + 1% rebate"), recommendation_text ("Counter with Option A, estimated cost ¥3,027.77"), href}`; no stages.
- `receivables_card`: `{outstanding_short, overdue_short, next_text ("Foshan, ¥420k in 32 days"), financing_note ("Part of this receipt will repay the ¥255k invoice financing; about ¥163k reaches your cash." when an advance is open on that invoice, from the trajectory's events on the due day, never computed here), href}`.
- `changes_card`: the two or three latest ledger sentences the home already words.
