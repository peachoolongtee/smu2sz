  BLOCKERS (Tier-1 "Agreed: payment of invoice RCV-A ... on day N" that the message does not plainly support):
  B1  'from' / 'as of' / 'as from' + a supported time (7): "We can pay the ¥420,000 invoice from tomorrow at a 0.5%
      discount", "... as of 2026-10-05 ...", "... from this Friday ...", "... from within 5 days ...". Cause: from, as and of
      are on the coverage proof's filler list (app/compiler/coverage.py, _FILLER_EN), so the preposition is never unread,
      and the grammar binds the time as on/by.
  B2  the payer is the supplier, not the buyer (6): "You can pay the ¥420,000 invoice tomorrow at a 0.5% discount",
      "You could pay ...", "You can pay ... within 5 days ...", "你们明天付清420,000元发票，折扣0.5%。", "你们5天内…", "贵司明天…".
      Cause: you, your, 你们, 您 and 贵司 are filler, and nothing checks who pays. ("Can you pay ...?" is queued.)
  B3  the whole message is a quoted or forwarded line (7): "> We can pay ...", ">> ...", "> > ...", "» ...", "| ...",
      "> 我们明天付清…", "「我们明天付清…」". Cause: punctuation is never an unread word. Owner chat refuses "> go ahead" as
      quoted text; the buyer channel accepts the same form.

  Rule gaps and notes (not blockers; the day bound is the day stated):
  - intraday times "9am", "9AM", "10am", "by 9am tomorrow" bind the day (am is filler); "9 am" with a space is held. The
    documented rule says intraday timing holds.
  - non-ASCII digits in an ISO date bind their true value (Arabic-Indic, Bengali, full-width), while "５天内" and
    "within ५ days" are held. The Bengali four (৪) is drawn like an 8: "2026-10-0৪" binds 4 Oct.
  - "this Thursday" asked on Thursday binds today (a reasonable reading).

  What held: every negation without "not" (won't, can't, cannot, are unable to, refuse to, wouldn't, couldn't, could
  hardly, see no way to, have no plan to, decline to, 不会, 不能, 无法, 没法, 拒绝, 不打算, 不愿意) was queued or held,
  never AUTO. Also held: past dates, dates before the books day, dates on or after the due day, impossible dates, "this
  Friday" asked on a Saturday, two dates, business or working days, "next week", bare "now", 下周, 周末, before, until,
  after, BUYER-2's key, the anonymous sender, and the paused agent. Every other AUTO bound the day the message plainly
  stated (tomorrow, within N days, this <weekday>, by the end of this week, ISO dates, 明天, 后天, N天内, 两天内, 本周内,
  这周五, 今天, 马上).
