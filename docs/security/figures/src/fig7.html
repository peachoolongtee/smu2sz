How to read itEach open or mitigated risk in section 04, rated for a production deployment as the code stands (NIST SP 800-30 style, three levels). The certified local demo is lower on every axis. Likelihood. Low: needs a privileged credential, database access or an unlikely sequence. Moderate: reachable by an authenticated non-owner or a realistic input. High: reachable anonymously. Impact. Low: no money moves; minor disclosure or inconvenience. Moderate: a bounded financial effect, or disclosure of confidential or personal data. High: money moved or commitments made beyond the mandate, or the audit trail falsified. Not on the map. Production blockers are prerequisites, not risks: SEC-06 (no production configuration), SEC-11 (one process only), no live-model certification, PostgreSQL not in CI, no backup, restore or emergency stop.
