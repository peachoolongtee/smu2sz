# Screenshots after the UI overhaul of 12 October 2026 (pattern "A1 · Seamless")

Taken in mock mode from the merged tree (`int/ui-overhaul`, the tree merged into `dev`), one window-height capture per file, never a
full-page capture (the sticky plan bar on Home makes those misleading). A page taller than the window has a second file, `-2`, one screen
further down. Names are `<page>-<state>-<width>-<lang>.png`; widths are 1280x800 and 390x844; Chinese is 1280 only, for Home, Cash,
Receivables and Approvals.

States: `day0` (the Demo's first day), `offer-evaluated` (the buyer's offer read and checked), `after-plan` (the funding plan ran),
`pending-day34` / `pending` (the day-34 cure waiting for approval), `day34` (the late invoice), `creditlineonly` (that row chosen on Cash),
`decided`.

Known in these captures: a Chinese page in mock mode shows English sentences on Cash, Home's deal rows and Receivables (those mock
recordings are English only; the live app sends the language). The Deals page is the 12 October reskin in the new shell: its redesign
waits for the owner's choice between the two mockups in `docs/design/`.
