# UI audit, 16 October 2026 (branch `fix/ui-audit`)

**How.** Live mode (API on 8051 in memory with the demo scenarios, web on 3151, `NEXT_PUBLIC_DEMO=1`) and mock mode (web on 3151,
`NEXT_PUBLIC_API_MODE=mock`), driven by Playwright scripts (this session had no browser MCP tools). Each page was loaded at a width and
language and checked by script for horizontal page scroll, elements past the right edge, raw tokens (`None`, `null`, `undefined`, `NaN`,
`OFF-…`, HTTP codes, `[object Object]`, ENGINE_CODES), English lines on a Chinese screen, open spinners, open dialogs (and where they sit),
console errors and failed requests, then screenshots were read by eye.

**Coverage.** 19 routes (Home, Deals, a deal, Documents, Cash, What-if, Capex, Receivables, Activity, Demo, Setup, Approvals, Security,
Ledger, Evidence, Customer, Decision record, Chat, Trust) on the busy books at 1440 en, 390 en and 1024 zh (57 loads); the same 19 in mock
at 1440 en and 390 zh (38 loads); the six-scene golden story walked end to end in live (12 steps, the owner's approval pressed by hand);
blank books and the setup wizard steps 1 to 6 at 1440, 1024, 390 and 360; the header at 800, 900, 1000, 1024 and 1100 in en and zh,
signed in and out; the Load dialog at 1024 scrolled. Own books were checked through the API test (opening real books through the wizard
needs a full draft; the server wording is pinned by `test_the_story_names_blank_and_own_books_as_what_they_are`). Short facilities was
loaded and reset but not swept page by page (time-box).

Screenshots are in the session scratchpad, not in the repo; the column names the shot (`<folder>/<name>`).

Severity: **blocker** (a judge cannot go on), **judge-visible** (a judge sees it and it looks broken or wrong), **minor**.
